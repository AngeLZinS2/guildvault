# Vice City at dusk

A genuine GTA VI sunset scene carries the atmosphere while the dashboard remains the functional foreground.

The five focal scenes are the night city, Leonida islands, daytime skyline, Jason and Lucia, and the airport at sunset. Preserve every source image's palette. Keep images full bleed; use cover on narrow viewports. No typography or added decorative roles belong in the background.

Motion uses the Hyperframes multi-phase-camera pattern simplified to a 4% push per image, with 1.2-second opacity dissolves adapted from transitions-dissolve. Five images span a 50-second cycle. The initial and final night scene match to avoid a loop jump. No screen shake, blur, strobing, generated particles or additional overlays in the composition.

Dashboard contrast belongs to the scoped host layer, independently of the reusable scene. No login styling changes.
